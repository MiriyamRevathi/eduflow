Super Admin Quick Guide

As Super Admin, you have full oversight across all institutions, users, financial ledgers, and academic reports.

  • Institutions: View, create, edit, approve, suspend, or delete schools and colleges.
  • Users & Roles: Manage RBAC permissions for all 12 platform user roles.
  • Finance: Track nationwide tuition fee collections and pending balances.
  • AI Risk Analytics: Inspect student dropout risk predictions generated by local Random Forest models.
